    How to play
    • Select an operator, then click a tile, or move the cursor with I/J/K/L and press Enter.
    • Click a weapon in an operator's inventory to select it: nobody fires without one. Right-click fires; hold the button to keep firing.
    • Shift+click on the ground adds a leg to the route. Keys 1-4 pick an operator, Shift+number adds one, 0 swaps one and all, Alt+1-8 press an inventory slot. Right-click a slot (or Alt+Shift+1-8) to drop that item on the ground; F picks it up again.
    • Stand on a relay, panel or server and press E to use it.
    • To finish an operation every operative still standing must be in the extraction zone (a car driven into it brings its riders).
    • Some operations ask you to disable a marked target or hold a position for a set time.
    • New game (on the operations screen) starts the whole campaign again after asking first; your settings stay.
    • Press A, or use Select all, to order the whole team at once.
    • Each operator's three coloured sliders set Adrenaline, Perception and Intelligence.
    • Drag, use the arrow keys or push the mouse to the map edge to pan; scroll or +/- to zoom, C to centre, or click the minimap.
    • Funds build up while you play. Spend them in Research & Armoury.
    • F loots a disabled enemy's gear. Each operator holds up to eight items.
    • Space pauses. Keys can be changed in Settings.
