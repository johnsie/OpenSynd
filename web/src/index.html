    OPEN CIVIC OPERATIONS NETWORK

    OpenSynd: First Light

    The Signal Yard has gone quiet. Two public relays that carried the district's voice have been cut off by remote administrators, and the service gate to the east is locked. Your four-person field team has one night to reconnect the district, unlock the gate, and leave before the watchers raise the alarm.

    • Select an operator, then click a tile or use the arrow keys and Enter to move.
    • Stand on a relay or panel and press E to restore it.
    • Disable watchers with the pulse emitter from close range; keep your team intact.
    • Press A, or use Select all, to order the whole team at once.
    • Use the Speed and Brain bars to train an operator: faster walking, quicker pulse.
    • The yard is large: drag to pan, scroll or +/- to zoom, C to centre on your team, or click the minimap.
    • Space pauses. Keys can be changed in Settings.
